How to Choose Comfortable Shapewear and Wireless Bras

By Curated4Her Team • Shapewear, Wireless Bras & Women's Apparel

Shapewear and wireless bras have become everyday wardrobe options for many women looking for comfortable foundations under different types of clothing.

Rather than focusing only on compression or appearance, choosing the right piece starts with fit, comfort, intended use and the clothing you plan to wear over it.

What Makes Shapewear Comfortable?

Comfort can depend on several factors.

Fit

The right size is one of the most important considerations. Shapewear that is too tight can be uncomfortable, while clothing that is too loose may not provide the intended level of smoothing.

Always check the manufacturer's sizing information before purchasing.

Compression

Different garments can provide different levels of compression.

Consider whether you're looking for light smoothing for everyday clothing or a more structured garment for a particular outfit.

Fabric

The feel and flexibility of the fabric can affect how comfortable a garment is during extended wear.

Consider breathability, stretch and the garment's construction.

Seams and Construction

Seams, edges and waistbands can affect how a garment sits under clothing.

If you're wearing shapewear beneath fitted clothing, a smooth construction may be particularly useful.

Different Types of Shapewear

Bodysuits

Bodysuits combine coverage across multiple areas in a single garment and can work particularly well under certain dresses and fitted outfits.

Shaping Shorts

Shaping shorts can provide coverage around the waist, hips and upper legs while working with dresses, skirts and other clothing.

High-Waisted Shapewear

High-waisted designs can extend higher around the midsection and may be useful under high-rise clothing or dresses.

Smoothing Underwear

Smoothing underwear provides a less extensive alternative when you want a streamlined foundation without a full-body garment.

Wireless Bras vs. Underwire Bras

Wireless bras use a different construction from traditional underwire designs.

Potential considerations include:

  • Comfort
  • Flexibility
  • Support
  • Shape
  • Intended clothing
  • Personal preference

Neither design is automatically right for everyone. The best choice depends on your individual fit and wardrobe needs.

How to Choose the Right Shapewear Size

Avoid automatically choosing a smaller size because you want more compression.

Instead:

  1. Take accurate measurements.
  2. Check the manufacturer's size chart.
  3. Consider the garment's intended compression.
  4. Think about how long you'll wear it.
  5. Make sure you can move comfortably.

A good fit should allow normal movement.

When Can You Wear Shapewear?

Everyday Clothing

Light smoothing pieces may work with everyday dresses, pants or skirts.

Professional Clothing

Some people choose smoothing garments underneath workwear when they want a streamlined foundation.

Dresses

Different shapewear designs can work with different dress silhouettes.

Special Occasions

For formal clothing, consider the garment's neckline, length, fabric and overall silhouette when choosing shapewear.

Frequently Asked Questions

How should shapewear fit?

Shapewear should fit securely while allowing comfortable movement. Follow the manufacturer's sizing recommendations rather than automatically choosing a smaller size.

Should you size down in shapewear?

Not necessarily. Choosing a smaller size can affect comfort and fit. Use the manufacturer's measurements and size chart.

Is wireless shapewear comfortable?

Comfort depends on the garment, fit, construction and individual preference. Wireless designs can be an option for people who prefer less structured bra construction.

What type of shapewear works under dresses?

The appropriate style depends on the dress. Consider the neckline, hemline, fabric and areas you want the garment to cover.

How do I choose a wireless bra?

Start with accurate measurements and consider the level of support, coverage, fabric and clothing you'll wear with it.

Can shapewear be worn every day?

Some people choose lightweight smoothing garments for everyday wear. Comfort and appropriate fit should be priorities.

How do I find the correct shapewear size?

Use your measurements and compare them with the manufacturer's current size chart.
