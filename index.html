Good to know

Questions, answered

What is Curated4Her?

Curated4Her is an independent women's fashion and beauty edit. We pick trending dresses, wigs, skincare, sunglasses and gifts from trusted online brands and write practical buying guides so you can shop with confidence.

Is Curated4Her a store?

No. We don't sell anything directly. Every Shop button takes you to the brand's own website, where you pay, get shipping details and handle returns.

How do you choose what to feature?

Editors look at fit, fabric and construction, ingredient lists, brand return policies and how often a piece can be re-worn or re-used. Brands can't pay to be included in our picks.

Do you earn money from links?

Some links are affiliate links (mostly through Awin). If you buy through them we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. It helps keep our guides free.

What are the trending pieces for fall 2026?

Velvet and jewel-tone occasion dresses, one-shoulder sequin cocktail looks, glueless lace wigs, skin-first minimalist routines and bold oversized sunglasses are the pieces our readers are shopping most this season.

Where do I check prices and sizes?

Prices, sizes and stock change often, so we always send you to the brand's live product page. Check the size guide there before ordering.
