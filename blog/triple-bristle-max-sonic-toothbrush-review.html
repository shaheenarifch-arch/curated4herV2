How a 3-sided toothbrush works

A regular toothbrush cleans one surface at a time, so you have to angle it to reach the outside, the inside and the chewing surface of every tooth. Most people rush the inner surfaces, especially behind the lower front teeth.

The Triple Bristle head wraps around each tooth with three sets of angled bristles. As you move along your teeth, the front, back and top are brushed together. Triple Bristle was created by a dentist, and the Max is named after him. The sonic motor adds fast vibrations on top of your own brushing motion.

Features that matter

Four cleaning modes

Deep clean is for your everyday routine. Gentle clean and sensitive are useful for tender gums or the first week while you adjust. Massage mode is intended for the gums.

2-minute smart timer

Dentists commonly recommend brushing for two minutes. The built-in timer takes the guesswork out, which is helpful for anyone who usually stops early.

Travel-friendly charging

There is no bulky charging stand. The handle charges with a USB-C cord and a charging brick, and the maker says one charge lasts up to a month. That makes it easy to pack for trips.

Gentle on dental work

The soft, charcoal-infused bristles are designed to be gentle on teeth, gums and dental work. Triple Bristle says the Max is safe for braces, implants, crowns and veneers.

Waterproof design

You can use it in the shower, which helps if mornings are rushed.

Limitations and checks before you buy

  • Adjustment period: the 3-sided head feels different at first. Triple Bristle notes it may take some getting used to.
  • Replacement heads: check the price and availability of replacement brush heads, because that is the long-term running cost.
  • Specific dental conditions: if you have gum disease, recent surgery or very sensitive teeth, ask your dentist which mode and brushing pressure to use.
  • Returns and warranty: read the current return policy and warranty terms on the product page before ordering.

Getting started with a 3-sided sonic brush

  1. Charge the handle fully with the included USB-C cord before first use.
  2. Start on gentle or sensitive mode for the first few days.
  3. Place the head over your teeth so the three sides wrap around them, and guide it slowly along each arch. Let the vibrations do the work rather than scrubbing.
  4. Let the 2-minute timer finish, then use the included tongue cleaner.
  5. Switch to deep clean once the brush feels natural.
