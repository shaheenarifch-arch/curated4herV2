Sizing Cheat Sheet

  • Measure your waist at the narrowest point, usually just above the navel, without pulling the tape tight.
  • Measure your hips around the fullest part of your hips and seat for an accurate hip figure.
  • Read a brand's size chart by matching your actual measurements to its ranges, not your usual dress size.
  • If you fall between two sizes, consider whether you prefer a snugger or looser fit and size accordingly.
  • Sizing varies by brand, so always check each brand's own size chart before ordering leakproof underwear.

How to choose

  1. Measure yourself before shopping. Use the waist and hip steps in the sizing cheat sheet above so you can match your body to a brand's actual chart, which reduces the risk of ordering a size that does not fit your usual lingerie size.
  2. Compare the rise to your wardrobe needs. A high-waist style sits differently under leggings or fitted dresses than a lower rise, so matching rise to your typical outfits prevents visible lines or an uncomfortable fit.
  3. Match color to how visible the garment will be. Beige or nude tones are generally chosen for lighter clothing, while darker tones suit darker outer layers, which helps avoid an underwear line showing through fabric.
  4. Read the fabric and care details on the product page. Confirm washing instructions and fabric composition before buying, since different fabrics may need different care routines and this affects how long the garment performs as expected.
  5. Check the retailer's size chart, not just the product description. Marketing copy describes fit and feel, but only the brand's own size chart gives numeric guidance, which lowers the chance of a poor fit on arrival.
  6. Review the return and exchange policy before purchase. Intimate apparel policies can differ from other clothing categories, so understanding the policy in advance avoids an unwanted item you cannot return.
  7. Consider ordering one piece before buying multiples. Trying a single item first lets you confirm fit and comfort before committing to more of the same style, which reduces the risk of multiple unusable items.

Common buying mistakes

  • Choosing a size based on regular underwear instead of the brand chart. This can lead to a poor fit; always match your measurements to the specific size chart listed on the product page.
  • Assuming all high-waist styles fit identically. Rise height and stretch can vary between listings even within the same brand; check each product's own description rather than assuming consistency.
  • Overlooking fabric or care instructions. Skipping this can shorten how well the garment holds up; review the retailer's stated care details before washing.
  • Buying multiple colors or styles at once without trying one first. This raises the risk of needing to return several items; start with a single piece when trying a brand for the first time.
  • Ignoring the return policy for intimate apparel. Some policies restrict returns on this category; confirm the policy before checkout so you are not left with an unwanted item.
  • Judging absorbency or protection level from marketing language alone. Descriptive terms like "dependable protection" are marketing claims, not measured specifications; verify any capacity details directly with the retailer if that information matters to you.
  • Not checking for separate listings that look similar. As with the Nude Rise and High Waist listings, near-identical descriptions can hide small differences, so compare both pages before choosing.

Frequently asked questions

Is leakproof underwear the same as regular underwear in sizing?

Leakproof underwear generally follows the same sizing structure as regular underwear, using waist and hip measurements against a brand's size chart. However, the added fabric layers can affect stretch and fit slightly differently than non-absorbent styles, so it helps to check the specific brand's chart rather than assuming your usual size applies exactly.

Can I wear leakproof underwear every day?

Many styles are marketed for everyday wear, including the high-waist options described in this guide. Whether a specific pair suits daily use depends on the fabric and care instructions listed on the product page, so review those details and follow the suggested washing routine to help maintain the garment over time.

How do I choose between a high-waist and other rise options?

Choosing rise comes down to what you plan to wear over the underwear. A high-waist style is commonly chosen for coverage under fitted clothing or for a smoothing effect, while other rises may suit different outfit types. All three options featured here use a high-waist design, so compare rise preferences against your typical wardrobe before buying.

Does color affect how leakproof underwear performs?

Color itself does not change the described protective features; it mainly affects visibility under clothing. Nude or beige tones, such as the Nude Rise style, are generally chosen to stay less visible under lighter or sheer fabrics, while darker tones like Black Pearl suit darker outer layers.

Should I buy lace styles if I want maximum coverage?

Lace styles like Midnight Lace are built on the same base design as other EverLeakProof US options, according to the retailer description, but the listing does not specify how the lace trim affects edge coverage. If coverage is your main priority, compare the lace style's description closely against a plain high-waist option before deciding.

How do I know what size to order if I am between sizes?

If your measurements fall between two sizes on a brand's chart, consider your fit preference, since sizing up typically gives a looser feel while sizing down gives a snugger fit. Review any notes on the product page about fit, and keep in mind that sizing varies by brand, so checking the specific chart each time is the most reliable approach.

Can I buy more than one style from EverLeakProof US at once?
